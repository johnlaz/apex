Know how far to trust it

Every report says how it was made.

A banner at the top of each report tells you the basis of the number, so a rough estimate never looks like a market quote.

LIVE-SOURCED

At least two comparable listings found, each price verified against the retrieved page.

PARTIAL

Research ran, but fewer than two comps survived verification. Treat the range as softer.

ESTIMATE ONLY

Live research failed. The number is the AI's general knowledge, labeled as such.

LEGACY SNAPSHOT

Saved by an older version. Shown with its age, never presented as current.

How it works

Scan. Analyze. Track.

Evidence first, AI second.

Identify

Scan a VIN barcode, snap a photo, or type year, make and model. APEX decodes the VIN and fills in the details.

Research

It searches the live web for comparable listings and queries the official NHTSA recall database at the same time.

Verify

The AI may only use what was retrieved. Prices that don't appear in the source text are dropped, and wild numbers are re-anchored to the verified median.

What's real

When APEX can't measure it, it says "No data."

Real: retrieved or computed

  • Comparable listings that passed verification
  • NHTSA recall records
  • Dealer margin (retail minus trade-in)
  • Report date, from your device clock

AI knowledge: always labeled

  • General vehicle context and ownership notes
  • Estimate-only price ranges, flagged
  • Photo identification
  • Dealer form links, flagged unverified

Never shown

  • Made-up KBB, Edmunds, Manheim or NADA values
  • Invented days-on-market or inventory counts
  • Made-up regional stats
  • Anything stamped with the year the app was built

Private by design

No account. No server. No tracking.

Your key stays put

You bring your own free Groq key. It's stored in your browser and sent only to Groq.

Your data stays local

Garage, saved searches and settings live on your device. Backups are yours to export, and keys are left out unless you opt in.

Works offline

Install it to your home screen. The app shell works without a connection; research needs one.
