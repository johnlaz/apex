Process

Scan. Analyze. Track.

01
Point & Scan

Open APEX and aim at any vehicle. Our real-time viewfinder overlays a targeting reticle as the AI processes shape, color, badge geometry, and grille patterns simultaneously.

02
Instant Analysis

Within seconds, APEX returns a full identification card: make, model, year, trim, engine, production numbers, and a visual damage map with color-coded severity zones.

03
Deep Intelligence

Tap any result to drill into live market comps, historical auction data, owner forums, known recall history, and estimated ownership costs — all in one place.

04
Track & Alert

Save vehicles to your Garage and set custom price thresholds. APEX notifies you the moment a watched car hits your target — so you never miss a deal.
